# Privacy Policy — NexusDesk AI Public Beta

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data**: Email, hashed passwords, and display name.
- **Device Metadata**: Device display name, OS version, public key fingerprint (never private keys).
- **Session Telemetry**: Session start/end timestamps, connection states, and aggregate bandwidth.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not store plaintext screen video, keyboard keystrokes, clipboard history, or unapproved AI queries.
- Secret tokens, private keys, and passwords are encrypted on-device or redacted before transit.

## 3. Data Retention
- Ephemeral session state in Redis expires upon session termination.
- Consent-gated recordings are retained for 30 days in private encrypted storage.

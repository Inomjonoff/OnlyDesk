# Getting Started with NexusDesk AI

Welcome to NexusDesk AI — the high-performance, AI-assisted remote desktop platform.

## 1. Fast Track (3 Steps)

1. **Create an Account**: Register at [nexusdesk.uz](https://nexusdesk.uz).
2. **Install Desktop Agent**: Download `NexusDesk-v1.0.0-beta.1-setup.exe` from the Download page.
3. **Connect**: Share your 9-digit Device ID (e.g. `NXD-8821-9943`) with an authorized technician or connect to your own device.

---

## 2. Interactive Consent & Permissions

Every remote connection starts strictly in **Read-Only Screen View** mode. The remote participant cannot type, click, transfer files, or run AI tools until you explicitly check the permission boxes on the incoming connection dialog.

You can revoke permissions or end the session instantly at any time using the HUD bar at the top of your screen.
